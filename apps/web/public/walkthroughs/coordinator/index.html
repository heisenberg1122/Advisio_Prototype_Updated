Walkthrough transcript

1. Workspace and workflow setup

Welcome to ADVISIO. This walkthrough follows the Research Coordinator's tools, from workflow planning and researcher participation to academic records and defense results.

Sign in with your authorized coordinator account. The Research Coordinator uses the Course Professor workspace, identified by the Coordinator Portal badge. This demonstration uses fictional accounts and research records within an isolated local workspace.

Overview summarizes the research groups, submissions, and activities you monitor. The sidebar connects Workflow, Research Projects, Submissions, Class Record, Progress, and Defense. These areas have distinct responsibilities. Enrollment controls participation; a registered project tracks the study; and academic decisions are recorded against the applicable submission or defense cycle.

Open Workflow and choose Create another workflow. Enter a descriptive name and select the research type available within your assigned program. Save the workflow as a draft. A draft is a private configuration: saving it does not publish milestones or enroll researchers. We will continue with a prepared draft to examine its structure.

Topics organize related milestones. Milestones define the research steps, and document milestones contain submission requirements. Our prepared example begins with Adviser Selection, then Title Proposal, a proposal manuscript, and Defense Eligibility. This sequence is an example configured for the tutorial; departments can choose a different order. Inspect a milestone's instructions, submission mode, required files, and approval settings. A relative target, such as fourteen days, differs from a calendar target date. Required Milestone Condition identifies an earlier prerequisite. Defense Eligibility is an academic event type that provides an explicit coordinator approval gate for a later defense cycle.

Guides and Templates stores supporting resources for the workflow. Choose Upload resource, enter its title and description, and attach the demonstration guide. Save the resource and confirm that it appears in the list. These reference materials support the configured requirements; uploading a guide does not count as a researcher's submission.

Return to the workflow structure and review its summary. Topics, milestone assignments, and required submissions must be configured before publication. Select Publish, inspect the readiness information, and confirm when the workflow is ready. The published state makes the configured process available through its enrollment rules. Publication does not automatically approve participation requests or rewrite existing student submissions.

2. Researcher participation and monitoring

Participants separates Members, Join requests, and Invitations. Generate a workflow code or shared link. We now switch to a fictional researcher's account to request access using that code. The request is pending until the coordinator reviews it. Return to the coordinator account, open Join requests, and approve the prepared request. The researcher then appears in Members. Direct invitations are a separate option for registered researchers, and the recipient must accept before enrollment becomes active. Joining a workflow and registering a research title remain separate steps.

This prepared, enrolled group is at Adviser Selection. In the group leader's account, open the milestone and send a request to an eligible adviser. The request remains pending. We switch to the adviser's own account to review and accept it, then return to coordinator monitoring. The accepted assignment updates the recorded milestone progress. The coordinator monitors this process; the coordinator account is not used to make the adviser's decision.

At Title Proposal, the group leader submits the required proposed titles. Switch back to the coordinator account and open the title review. Read the alternatives and use feedback when a revision is needed. For the prepared acceptable proposal, select a title and approve it. The approved title registers the research project while preserving the existing group's workflow, members, adviser assignment, and recorded progress. Approval is an academic review decision, rather than a routine registration shortcut.

Research Projects lists registered studies within the workflows you oversee. Locate the approved demonstration title and inspect its project and workflow information. Open Progress to compare the current milestones and recorded completion states. Pending requirements indicate where further work or guidance is needed. The configured conditions still determine which milestone the group can start next.

Open Submissions and its Review Queue. The workspace shows the exact submitted document beside comments and review controls. Select a passage in this text-based PDF, attach a comment, and post it. A posted comment is saved independently of an academic decision. Enter decision feedback and request revision when the submission needs further work.

Here is a separate prepared revised submission. Inspect its submitted version and earlier attempt history. Adviser and other mandatory reviewer approvals apply to the exact version being reviewed. Once those checks are satisfied and the work meets the requirement, the coordinator can approve the submission. Any grade is recorded and released separately; an approval decision does not itself publish a score.

3. Class record and attendance

Class Record uses the active researcher roster of an owned workflow. The periods are Prelim, Midterm, and Finals. Individual activities, eligible linked milestone scores, and attendance support the recorded calculations. Review the prepared activity scores and attendance marks, make the demonstration correction, and save the private drafts. Saving does not release grades. When required entries and calculations are complete, Release period publishes a snapshot for the active roster. The researcher's My Grades view then shows that researcher's released result. Later draft edits remain private, and corrections create a new recorded release rather than rewriting the earlier snapshot.

4. Defense management and role handoffs

Defense preparation begins with the configured Defense Eligibility milestone. Review the group's prerequisites and required submissions. This prepared group is ready for coordinator approval. Approve its eligibility and inspect the updated status. The approval belongs to this group and milestone for one defense cycle. Adviser acceptance or completion of unrelated workflow activities does not replace this explicit approval.

Request the Dean signature for a group with approved, unused eligibility. Select the authorized dean, review the subject and message, and attach the prepared PDF letter. Send the request and keep its pending status distinct from approval. We then switch to the dean's own account, where the request is reviewed and signed. Scheduling requires that valid signed letter to be linked to this same eligibility cycle; an earlier defense letter cannot unlock a later cycle.

This separate prepared group already has approved eligibility and its corresponding signed letter. Create a defense event, select the eligible group, and enter the schedule, venue, and session details. Assign a panel chair and the required panelist, then choose Create Event and Invite. Invitations begin pending. We inspect the responses through the participants' own accounts before continuing with preparation. Creating an event does not mean every participant has accepted.

Open the session's defense documents. Select the approved, immutable manuscript submitted for its Defense Eligibility milestone. Configure the structured form and inspect the researcher roster, rating levels, individual presentation criteria, and group manuscript criteria. Review the scoring weights and publication requirements. Once the required participants and materials are ready, publish the packet. Publication preserves the selected manuscript and evaluation configuration for authorized panelists; it does not submit their evaluations or release an official result.

When the session conditions are satisfied, the coordinator can start the live defense. We then open an accepted panelist's own workspace. The panelist reviews the prepared ratings, previews the evaluation, confirms it, and submits the final record. Panelists make these decisions through their own accounts. The coordinator cannot complete a missing panelist evaluation by selecting an official outcome.

We now use a separate prepared session with all required evaluations finalized. Inspect the consolidated individual and manuscript results and verify the recorded evaluation history. Select the official decision separately from the calculated scores. Enter revision instructions when required, then confirm and release the result. The released outcome controls the group's next steps. A passed defense consumes that cycle's eligibility; revisions or another defense follow their recorded requirements.

5. Communication and ongoing monitoring

Announcements sends an update to active participants in the selected workflow. Choose the workflow, review the demonstration message and audience, and publish it. Messages supports group collaboration, while Inbox handles formal correspondence. Calendar, Manuscript Reviews, and Official Copies provide related records under your account's permissions. Return to Progress to monitor the researchers' next steps after each decision.

That concludes the ADVISIO Research Coordinator walkthrough. The example connects workflow planning, participation, research review, academic records, and defense coordination. Return to any chapter when you need to review a specific responsibility.
