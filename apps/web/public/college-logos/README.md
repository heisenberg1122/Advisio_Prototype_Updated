# University college logos

Official University of the Assumption logos, downloaded from the images linked on
[Schools and Colleges](https://web.ua.edu.ph/academics/). These local assets keep
the profile identity available without contacting an external image service.

Source directory: `https://web.ua.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/`

`cit.png` is the College of Information Technology's updated seal, taken from
`https://www.ua-cit.com/cit_logo.png` (1080×1080, SHA-256
`37551fe158b220cf9a435551f9e97966f0cc2eed8050eda912b9f84d46c89260`). Its empty
transparent margin was trimmed to the seal and the result scaled to 300×300, so
the seal fills the image like the other files. It replaces the earlier
`UA-CITCLS` logo.

Logos belong to the University of the Assumption and their respective schools
and colleges. Apart from that resize, no modifications were made to the
supplied images.

All files are cropped to the seal itself. Keep any replacement cropped the same
way: the dashboard banner sizes the seal from the image edges.
