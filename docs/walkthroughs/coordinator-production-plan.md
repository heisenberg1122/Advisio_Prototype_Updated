# Research Coordinator recording

The master follows 19 numbered clips organized into five reusable chapters: workspace and workflow setup; researcher participation and monitoring; class record and attendance; defense management; and communication with closing. Welcome/sign-in and initial/revised submissions use separate cached beats. Narration is measured at about 9 minutes 46 seconds, below the ten-minute limit.

The role is the Research Coordinator, using the Course Professor workspace and Coordinator Portal identity. The example sequence is configurable, not a mandatory institutional research order. Topics contain milestones; document milestones contain requirements. Relative targets and calendar dates are explained separately.

## Production efficiency

Reuse the established narrator, pronunciation dictionary, branding, fixed recording viewport, measured captions, Remotion frame design, and native stationary-video compositor. Rehearse interactions before timed capture. Save each successful beat so recovery can resume without repeating successful captures. Narration caches are separate by voice settings and role. Existing Researcher and Dean outputs remain separate.

The master has five chapter ranges that can be exported from the same finished 1080p video using `--chapters`. Corrections replace only affected beats and cached compositions. The hybrid compositor is used only for stationary overview framing; it rejects camera or diagram sequences instead of silently omitting them.

The native compositor fingerprints the source design, exact beat metadata, recorded clip, narration bytes, logo, and timeline dimensions. A matching completed segment is reused only after its measured duration is checked. Paid narration is not regenerated by capture, preview, render, or verification commands. The approved narrator and unchanged text remain cached.

## Isolated environment

`coordinator-server.mjs` creates a new `walkthrough_coordinator_<random>` schema in the dedicated local demo cluster at `127.0.0.1:5436/advisio_evaluation`. API and web bind to loopback ports 5300 and 3300. External integration credentials are blank; email and background workers are disabled. Normal account authentication, college boundaries, academic permissions, eligibility checks, signature password confirmation, evaluation verification, and grade-release controls remain active. Nothing is published to production.

`seed-coordinator.mjs` creates fictional identities, program records, a published monitoring workflow, a prepared lifecycle draft, enrolled researcher examples, adviser/title participation records, PDF submissions, a reference guide, and three separate defense scenarios. `prepare-coordinator.mjs` uses each authorized role's real API to approve eligibility, obtain the matching Dean signature, create invitations, accept as panelists, publish the packet, and finalize evaluations. An independent release-ready example does not share the session used to demonstrate scheduling. Private class-record grades and attendance remain unreleased until recorded through the UI.

Credentials and fixture identifiers are saved only under ignored `.walkthrough/coordinator/`. Cross-role browser navigation uses that role's own login token. Prepared scenarios are labelled in the narration. The recorder does not grant extra roles, impersonate another role through coordinator permissions, alter academic decisions for real records, or manufacture successful status changes in animation.

## Gates shown in the tutorial

1. Draft creation remains distinct from publishing.
2. Code-based join requests require coordinator approval; direct invitations require recipient acceptance.
3. The researcher requests an adviser; the adviser accepts through the adviser account.
4. The leader submits proposed titles; approval promotes the existing participation record.
5. Comments, approval/revision decisions, and grade release are separate actions tied to the applicable version.
6. Class grades remain private drafts until explicitly released. Prelim, Midterm, Finals, attendance, and the researcher's own released grade are shown.
7. Defense eligibility is approved for the group and current milestone cycle.
8. A valid Dean signature must be linked to that exact eligibility before scheduling.
9. Invitation creation is distinct from participant acceptance.
10. Packet publication preserves the approved manuscript, roster, rubric, and panel assignments.
11. Panelists submit their own evaluations. The coordinator cannot supply a missing evaluation by choosing an outcome.
12. Official result release requires finalized evaluations and the retained, verified evaluation checkpoint. Calculated scores and panelist recommendations do not automatically choose that outcome.
